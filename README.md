# Al-Ala Education Foundation (Welfare Wing)

Official website for **Al-Ala Education Foundation**, the welfare wing of **Al-Ala International School & Education System**. 

Designed and moulded in the **Azwedo Amsterdam (Made in Webflow)** creative editorial design system with high-contrast monochrome, mint green accents (`#81d98e`), bold typography (Manrope), 2px solid borders, and structured multi-page navigation.

## ✨ Highlights & Features

- **Multi-Page Editorial Architecture**:
  - `index.html`: Hero with dynamic fieldwork photo carousel, directional cinematic scrim, prominent partner logos, quick giving calculator, interactive sticky domain cards, philosophy, and spotlights.
  - `about.html`: 20+ years heritage, leadership profile, mission/vision, and 2026–27 strategic expansion objectives.
  - `programs.html`: Dedicated pages for all 8 official welfare programs with balanced image framing, snapshot pills (Target, Scope, Impact), and full checklists.
  - `impact.html`: Real case studies and interactive modal photo lightbox gallery.
  - `zakat.html`: 100% Shariah-compliant Zakat information with an interactive 2.5% calculator across gold, silver, cash, and investments.
  - `donate.html`: Dedicated donation center covering all 8 programs + Zakat with one-click bank account copy (Meezan Bank, HBL, Raast, Roshan Digital Account) and pledge voucher generation.
  - `contact.html`: Interactive contact form, campus visit details, direct phone/WhatsApp, and school location map.
- **Multi-Currency Support**: Instant live conversion between PKR (Rs.), USD ($), GBP (£), EUR (€), and SAR (SR).
- **100% Zero-Dependency Stack**: Pure vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript. Fast, lightweight, and zero build step required.
