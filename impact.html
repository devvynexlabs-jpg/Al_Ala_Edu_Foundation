RELIEF COLLABORATIONS

Alliances in compassion.

Al-Ala Foundation actively collaborates with established welfare institutions to ensure maximum reach, accountability, and impact.

Shaukat Khanum Memorial Cancer Hospital

Regular financial charity disbursements and specialized oncology medicine subsidies for impoverished cancer patients fighting terminal illnesses.

Edhi Homes & Welfare Foundation

Periodic food ration drives, grocery staples, and clothing distribution to residents of Edhi shelter homes across the province.

Kashana Social Welfare Society

Transitional welfare, wedding support assistance, and winter clothing packages for young destitute women and orphan girls.

Chaman Institute of Special Children

Specialized educational materials, wheelchair mobility devices, and clothing collection drives tailored for differently-abled children.
