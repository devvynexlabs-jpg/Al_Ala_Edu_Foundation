OUR IDENTITY & ROOTS

About Al-Ala Foundation

Born from over two decades of educational leadership at Al-Ala International School, our foundation bridges academic excellence with compassionate, transparent social welfare.

THE GENESIS

Education as the ultimate vehicle for social justice.

Alhamdulillah, during the academic year 2025–2026, Al-Ala Education Foundation continued its commitment to serving society across education, welfare, community development, and humanitarian initiatives.

As part of the Al-Ala Education System, the foundation operates on the conviction that poverty should never deny a child the right to quality schooling, STEM labs, or moral development. Administrative costs are subsidized by the school, allowing 100% of your Zakat and charitable contributions to be disbursed directly to beneficiaries.

Strategic Objectives 2026–2027

02

03

06
Build strategic partnerships with community organizations and stakeholders
