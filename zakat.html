HOW YOUR ZAKAT IS MANAGED

A sacred Amanah, handled with transparent accountability.

Zakat is a religious pillar designed to eradicate poverty and support those unable to support themselves. Al-Ala Education Foundation manages Zakat in strict accordance with classical Islamic jurisprudence.

100% Zakat Disbursement Policy

All administrative overheads, salaries, and operating utilities are fully covered by institutional patrons, ensuring 100% of your Zakat reaches verified recipients.

Rigorous Case Verification

Every beneficiary (orphan student, single mother, cancer patient) is verified by our senior Welfare Board before funds are released.

Dedicated Islamic Bank Account

Zakat funds are maintained in a segregated Islamic banking account at Meezan Bank to prevent commingling with general interest-free charity.

COMMONLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Zakat & Shariah Guidelines

Can Zakat be used to pay school fees for orphan students?

Yes. Scholars across Islamic jurisprudence agree that providing education, books, and uniforms to impoverished orphans falls under the category of empowering the needy (Fuqara and Masakeen).

Can Zakat be allocated to cancer patients?

Yes. Impoverished cancer patients unable to afford chemotherapy and diagnostics are eligible recipients of Zakat under our collaboration with Shaukat Khanum Memorial Cancer Hospital & Hameed Maggo Trust.

Can overseas Pakistanis pay Zakat via Roshan Digital Account?

Yes. We have dedicated Roshan Digital Account and international SWIFT IBAN credentials at Meezan Bank allowing donors from the UK, USA, Gulf, and Europe to transfer Zakat directly without foreign remittance complications.

Do I receive an official tax and Zakat certificate?

Yes. Upon transferring your Zakat and sharing your transaction receipt or screenshot via WhatsApp or email, our finance office generates an official signed and stamped donation certificate for your tax and personal records.
