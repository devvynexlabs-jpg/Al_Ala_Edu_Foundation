FIELDWORK INITIATIVES

Our Welfare Programs

Eight dedicated sectors of compassionate service empowering vulnerable families, orphans, and students across Pakistan through education, healthcare, and humanitarian relief.

01 / CHILD WELFARE

Child Welfare & Moral Development

Target: Orphans & Needy Scope: 100% Free Schooling Impact: 1,200+ Scholarships

Dedicated to the moral, spiritual, and educational nourishment of children who have lost guardians or means of support, ensuring no deserving child is left behind.

Free Education Program – Every Child Deserves to Learn
Scholarships and fee concessions for orphans and teachers' children
Distribution of free books, stationery, bags, and educational supplies
Nutritional meal program and regular child pediatric health camps
Clothing collection and distribution for Chaman Institute of Special Children

02 / EDUCATIONAL EXCELLENCE

Educational Welfare & STEM Learning

Target: Students & Teachers Scope: STEM Labs & Girl Child Impact: 50+ Workshops Held

Education is the cornerstone of the Foundation's mission. We equip underprivileged students and teachers with 21st-century modern technology, STEM labs, and professional training.

STEM Learning Workshops – Innovate, Create, Elevate
Girl Child Education – Educate a Girl, Empower a Nation
Teacher Training Initiative – Better Teachers, Brighter Futures
Digital learning access, adult literacy, and library development drive
Career counseling sessions and student mentorship programs
Support Education →
03 / SOCIAL RELIEF

Social Welfare & Food Security

Target: Vulnerable Families Scope: Ramadan & Emergency Impact: 15,000+ Rations

Providing vulnerable families with food security, nutritious meals, essential daily sustenance, and urgent emergency relief.

Community kitchen in Ramadan serving daily hot Iftar meals
Regular charity support for cancer patients through Shaukat Khanum
Ration support for cancer patients through Hameed Maggo Trust
Charity support & ration assistance to Edhi Homes & Kashana Social Welfare
Save the Earth campaign & community tree plantation drives

04 / HEALTHCARE

Health Welfare & Free Dental Camps

Target: Students & Public Scope: Dental & Clinical Camps Impact: 50+ Camps Conducted

Organizing regular healthcare clinics, free dental screenings, and specialized diagnostic checkups to ensure vital healthcare is accessible to all.

Free Dental Checkup Medical Camps with certified dentists
Free clinical medical checkups & prescription support for the needy
Women's health education & maternal health awareness sessions
Student health awareness sessions & healthy eating campaigns
Hygiene kits, dental supplies & medical prescription subsidies

05 / WOMEN WELFARE

Women Welfare & Skill Empowerment

Target: Mothers & Widows Scope: Quran & Vocational Impact: 200+ Women Enrolled

Empowering women through education, spiritual reflection, and vocational skills to establish economic independence, financial resilience, and confident parenting.

Mothers' workshops on family psychology and child Tarbiyyah
Weekly Quran reflection classes and intensive Dawrah-e-Quran sessions
Free vocational stitching, tailoring, and computer courses
Transitional welfare & emergency grants for widowed mothers
Empower a Mother →
06 / INCLUSIVE CARE

Elderly & Disabled Care Without Barriers

Target: Special Needs & Seniors Scope: Wheelchairs & Support Impact: Chaman Institute Ally

Extending support to vulnerable members of society through collaboration with specialized welfare organizations and assistive equipment distribution.

Elder friendship circles, Quran reflection & story-sharing sessions
Mobility assistance and wheelchair distribution for special needs
"Ability Beyond Disability" vocational life-skills workshops
Free consultancy and guidance services for the Al-Ala community
Support Mobility →
07 / LIVELIHOOD

Economic Welfare & Qarz-e-Hasana

Target: Orphan Brides & Artisans Scope: Jahez & Micro-Finance Impact: 100% Interest-Free

Supporting struggling families through interest-free micro enterprise financing, wedding assistance for needy brides, and clothing distribution to restore financial self-reliance.

Wedding support assistance for orphan and underprivileged brides
Interest-free loan support (Qarz-e-Hasana) for small family businesses
Community seasonal cloth drives & winter blanket packages
Vocational toolkits for micro-trades & home cottage setups
Emergency household debt relief to preserve family dignity

Humanitarian Outreach & Disaster Relief

Target: Calamity Victims Scope: Rations, Tents & Meds Impact: Gaza & Flood Relief

Mobilizing immediate humanitarian relief during times of calamity, including flood relief in Pakistan, earthquake response, and Gaza aid camps.

Emergency aid shipments and relief supplies for Gaza Relief Camps
Pakistan Flood Relief emergency rations and medical supplies
Earthquake relief assistance and temporary shelter materials
Student-led humanitarian drives and community youth volunteer initiatives
Direct collaboration with disaster relief networks and emergency logistics
